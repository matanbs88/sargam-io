Audio lessons:
- 14 techniques, multiple dynamics/RR, close ribbon and stereo room recordings.
- SustainNormal is plain sustained tone; Tongued has a more accented breath attack.
- Dynamics adjusts forcefulness as well as gain; one V1 layer cannot replicate it.
- Recorded breath vibrato and Kontakt smooth vibrato primarily change amplitude.
- Legato has recorded transition samples, pre-transition timing and crossfade.
- TACT configures triggers by note/velocity/CC/keyswitch/pedal; ornaments are separate.
- Dynamic low-pass filter, ADSR, release samples, mic mix and effects affect timbre.
- Practice playback should not add randomized ornaments without explicit choice.
